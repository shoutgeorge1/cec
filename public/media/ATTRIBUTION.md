# Media attribution

Product photography, collection thumbnails, and logos under `website/public/media/` were downloaded from the **Cutting Edge Creations** public storefront (cuttingedgecreations.com / Shopify CDN) for this client engagement’s marketing scaffold.

They remain client-owned assets. Mirrored locally so the Next.js site can render reliably in development without hotlinking every request. Prefer curated homepage + category heroes (roughly 15–40 files), not a full catalog dump.
