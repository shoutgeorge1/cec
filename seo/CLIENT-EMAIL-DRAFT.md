Hi team,

Thank you for the opportunity to review the public Cutting Edge Creations storefront. Below is a concise readout of what we found and a practical plan to strengthen conversion and paid/organic readiness — without disrupting the brand you already have on Shopify.

## What we found (high level)

1. **Quote path** — Visitors who follow “Request a Quote” style CTAs need a clear, structured way to submit commercial interest (business type, product family, quantity/timeline). Today the contact path is clearer than a dedicated quote capture on the quote URL. Fixing that is the highest-leverage conversion improvement before ads.

2. **Catalog hygiene** — The public catalog is large and strong on commercial units, but a meaningful share of products show **$0.00** pricing and a set of **clone**-style URLs look like duplicate/import leftovers. That undermines trust on product and landing pages and makes paid traffic risky until cleaned up.

3. **Ads / measurement** — We are **not recommending meaningful paid spend yet**. Public HTML did not clearly show verified GA4 / Meta pixel setup for conversion measurement. After quote repair, catalog cleanup, and tracking access, a tightly scoped branded + commercial pilot can be reconsidered.

4. **SEO baseline** — Homepage metadata and commercial landers can better match how buyers search (**commercial / for sale** bounce houses, water slides, obstacle courses) rather than local party-rental “near me” intent. We fully parsed a Keyword Planner export (**2,354 keywords**) to guide information architecture and PPC phasing.

## Proposed plan

### 1. Marketing site layer (Next.js) + Shopify as commerce source of truth
- Keep Shopify as the catalog and checkout system of record.
- Add a marketing frontend aligned to your current Paper theme (fonts, orange/blue palette, logo, product photography) so it feels like the same company.
- Strong **Request a Quote** capture, commercial category landers, and curated **starter packages** (honest Package / Talk-to-sales cards built from real products until native Shopify bundles exist).

### 2. SEO / PPC (when ready)
- **Phase 0–1:** Branded defense + primary commercial keywords (for-sale / commercial intent).
- Deprioritize pure rental “near me” clusters for this manufacturer surface.
- Hold non-branded scale until tracking and landing conversion are verified.

### 3. Shopify hygiene
- Resolve intentional quote-only vs accidental $0.00.
- Unpublish or merge clone handles.
- Align collection landing pages to commercial buying journeys.

### 4. Tracking + quote ops
- Confirm GA4, Meta (if used), and quote/lead destination (inbox, CRM, or email).
- Define the conversion event that would justify any paid pilot.

## Soft next steps / access that would help

When you are ready, the fastest unlocks are:

- **Shopify Admin** (or a collaborator account) for catalog cleanup + theme/metafield context  
- **Google Ads** admin or MCC invite to the existing account (prefer connecting to what you already have vs building blind)  
- Confirmation of who should receive web quote leads today  
- Optional: Meta Business access if social ads are in scope later  

No campaigns need to launch to start the quote + catalog work.

## Tone of partnership

This is a build-forward plan: stabilize conversion and measurement, then grow with intent that matches how commercial buyers actually search. Happy to walk through the X-Ray findings and the marketing scaffold on a short call.
